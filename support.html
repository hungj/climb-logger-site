Climbing Workout Logger

Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email climbworkoutlogger@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your app version (Settings → Version, at the bottom), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There's no account or cloud sync. See the privacy policy.

How do I free up storage?

Videos take the most space. Go to Settings → Manage Video Storage to see which climbs use the most and delete videos you no longer need.

The trim screen picked the wrong start or end of my climb.

Climb detection is a suggestion. Drag the yellow handles to adjust it, or tap Upload Full to keep the whole video.

If I delete the app, is my data gone?

Yes. Everything is stored in the app on your device, so deleting the app deletes it, unless you restore from an iPhone backup.
