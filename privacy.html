Climbing Workout Logger

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Climbing Workout Logger is built so that your training stays yours. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (workouts, exercises, sets, climbs, notes, templates) and any videos you record or import are stored only on your device, inside the app. There is no account, no server, and no cloud sync run by us. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or your computer, that backup may include the app's data, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party code or services.
  • No network connections: the app does not send anything anywhere.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only to record climbing videos when you choose to.
  • Photos: to import videos you pick, and to save a video to your library when you ask. The app only sees the videos you select.

Video analysis features (such as detecting when a climb starts and ends, or showing a body-pose skeleton) run entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
